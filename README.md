# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of an e-commerce dataset using **Power BI**. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and build an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders understand revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional sales, and order patterns.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, KPI development, data visualization, and business analysis**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions:

- How much total revenue is generated?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which states contribute the most to sales?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which month generated the highest revenue?
- How satisfied are customers based on review scores?
- How are orders distributed across different order-value ranges?

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Health & Beauty is the leading revenue category
Health & Beauty generated approximately **1.26M in revenue**, making it the strongest-performing product category.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient inventory and prioritize marketing campaigns for high-performing products within this category.

### 2. São Paulo dominates regional revenue
São Paulo (SP) generated approximately **5.2M in revenue**, significantly outperforming other states.

**Recommendation:** Continue strengthening the SP market while developing targeted strategies in other regions to reduce dependency on a single market.

### 3. Sales demonstrate strong overall growth
The monthly sales trend shows substantial growth over the analyzed period, with **November 2017 emerging as the best-performing month**.

**Recommendation:** Analyze seasonal demand and successful campaigns around high-performing months and apply similar strategies to future peak periods.

### 4. Customer satisfaction is generally high
The review-score analysis shows that **5-star ratings represent the largest share of customer reviews**.

**Recommendation:** Identify the factors contributing to positive customer experiences while investigating low-rated orders to further improve satisfaction.

### 5. Orders are concentrated in lower-value ranges
The Order Value Distribution shows that lower-value transactions occur considerably more frequently than high-value transactions.

**Recommendation:** Use product bundles, cross-selling strategies, and minimum-order incentives to increase average basket value.

---

## 🔎 Most Interesting Finding

One of the most interesting findings was the strong concentration of revenue in **São Paulo (SP)**. The state generated approximately **5.2M in revenue**, far ahead of other leading regions.

This indicates that sales are highly concentrated geographically and presents an opportunity to expand successful strategies into other states.
